How Often Should You Clean Your Sofa in Dubai?

By the Dima Laundry team, 12 November 2026

Most Dubai homes should vacuum a fabric sofa every week and have it deep cleaned once a year, or every six months with children, pets or a lot of sand coming in from the balcony. Clean it sooner if it smells, looks dull or has stains. Dima Laundry shampoos sofas at your home for AED 70 per seat.

Why sofas get dirty faster in Dubai

A sofa does not look dirty until it suddenly does. By then the fabric has been collecting months of things you cannot see.

  • Sand and dust. Fine dust gets in through balcony doors, windows and on clothes, and settles deep into the fabric and the cushions.
  • Sweat and skin oils. In the hot months people come in from outside and sit straight down. Arms, headrests and seat fronts take most of it.
  • Humidity. On humid days, damp in the air helps smells cling to fabric, and an air conditioned room that is closed up all day keeps them there.
  • Food and drink. In many homes the sofa is also where people eat, watch TV and host guests.

None of this is a sign of a dirty home. It is what happens to fabric that is used every day in this climate.

How often to clean your sofa

There are two kinds of cleaning, and you need both.

Every week: vacuum. Use the upholstery tool on the seats, the back, the arms and down the sides of the cushions, where sand and crumbs collect. Lift the cushions out if they come off. This is the single best thing you can do to make a sofa last.

Once a year: deep clean. For a sofa used by a couple or a small household, one professional shampoo a year is a sensible starting point.

Every six months: deep clean in busier homes. Move to twice a year if any of these apply:

  • You have young children or pets.
  • The sofa is light coloured.
  • It is the main seat in the house and used every day.
  • Your balcony doors are often open and sand blows in.
  • Someone in the house smokes, or you cook with strong spices in an open plan space.

It is also worth booking a clean before you host guests for Eid or a family visit, and before you hand back a rented apartment with furnished items.

Signs your sofa needs a clean now

Do not wait for the calendar if you notice any of these:

  • A stale or musty smell when you sit down, even after airing the room.
  • Arms and headrests that look darker or shiny compared with the rest of the fabric.
  • Seat cushions that have turned a shade darker than the back.
  • Stains from food, drink, makeup or pets that have not come out with blotting.
  • Dust that puffs up when you pat a cushion.

Older stains are harder to treat than fresh ones, so when you book, tell the team what caused any marks and roughly how long they have been there.

What to do between deep cleans

  • Blot spills straight away. Use a clean white cloth and press, do not rub. Rubbing spreads the stain and pushes it deeper.
  • Check the care label first. Most sofas have a code under a cushion or on the frame. If you are not sure what it means, do not soak the fabric with water or cleaning sprays.
  • Turn and swap cushions. Flip and rotate them every few weeks so they wear and fade evenly.
  • Keep it out of strong sun. Afternoon sun through Dubai windows fades fabric quickly. Use blinds or curtains at the hottest time of day.
  • Use a throw. A washable throw on the seat people use most, or where a pet sleeps, takes much of the daily wear.

If your sofa has removable covers, you can send just the covers for cleaning between full shampoos.
