Frequently asked questions

Does Dima Laundry offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Dima Laundry collects from homes and offices across Dubai. Book online, on WhatsApp, by phone or in the Dima app.

How much does Dima Laundry charge?

Cleaning and pressing starts at AED 14 for a shirt, AED 48 for a two piece suit and AED 20 for an abaya or kandura. Pressing only starts at AED 9.80.

Where is Dima Laundry located?

Dima Laundry has 8 branches in Dubai: Dubai Marina (Marinascape and Crown), Al Sufouh, Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, Sheikh Zayed Road, Karama and Damac Aykon City, all open 10am to 10pm daily.

Does Dima Laundry clean clothes in-house?

Yes. Every item is cleaned in Dima Laundry's own processing centres in Al Quoz, which run 24 hours.

Does Dima Laundry serve businesses?

Yes. Dima Laundry provides commercial laundry for hotels, holiday homes, restaurants, salons, spas, gyms, clinics and corporate uniforms.

Does Dima Laundry have an app?

Yes. The Dima Laundry app is free on iPhone and Android for booking pickups, tracking orders and paying.
