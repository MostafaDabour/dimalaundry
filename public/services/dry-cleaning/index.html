Frequently asked questions

How much does dry cleaning cost in Dubai?

At Dima Laundry, dry cleaning and pressing costs AED 14 for a shirt, AED 15 for trousers, AED 48 for a two piece suit and AED 40 for a long dress. Premium service costs more and gives each piece extra care.

Does dry cleaning remove stains?

Dima Laundry treats stains before cleaning. Fresh stains have the best chance of full removal, so bring stained items in as soon as you can and point the stain out at drop off.

Will dry cleaning shrink my suit?

No. Dry cleaning uses no water, so wool and tailored garments keep their shape.

Can I get same day dry cleaning?

Express service is available. Ask on WhatsApp or at the counter for today's cut-off time.
