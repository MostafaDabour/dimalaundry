Simple, Transparent Laundry Pricing

Dima Laundry prices start at AED 14 to clean and press a shirt, AED 48 for a two piece suit and AED 20 for an abaya or kandura, with pressing only from AED 9.80. Prices are the same at every branch and were updated on 12 October 2026. For specialist garments, heavy stains, delicate items or restoration work, our team confirms the price before cleaning.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to dry clean a suit in Dubai?

At Dima Laundry, cleaning and pressing a two piece suit costs AED 48, or AED 80 with premium service for men's suits.

How much is ironing in Dubai?

Pressing only at Dima Laundry starts at AED 9.80 for a shirt and AED 10.50 for trousers.

What is the difference between standard and premium service?

Premium service gives each piece extra care and finishing. Ask the team what is included for your items.

Are prices the same at every branch?

Yes. Dima Laundry uses one price list across all Dubai branches.

How can I pay?

Pay at the counter, on delivery or in the Dima Laundry app. Commercial accounts are invoiced monthly.
