Laundry Pickup and Delivery in Dubai: How It Works

By the Dima Laundry team, 23 November 2026

Laundry pickup and delivery in Dubai works in four steps: you book on WhatsApp, by phone, online or in an app, a driver collects from your home or office, the clothes are cleaned and pressed, and they come back to your door. Dima Laundry collects across Dubai and cleans every item in its own Al Quoz centres.

How pickup and delivery works, step by step

Most Dubai laundries that collect follow the same basic pattern. At Dima Laundry it looks like this:

  1. Book. Schedule a pickup online, on WhatsApp, by phone or in the Dima app.
  2. We collect. The team collects your laundry from your home or workplace. In towers, that can be from your lobby or concierge.
  3. We clean. Every item is tagged, inspected, cleaned, pressed and quality checked in Dima's own Al Quoz centres. Nothing is sent out to a third party.
  4. We deliver. Your clothes come back clean and ready to wear.

You do not need to sort anything before the driver arrives. Everyday laundry is sorted by colour and fabric at the centre and never mixed with other customers' loads.

  • Phone: 800 DIMA (3462).
  • App: the Dima Laundry app is on the App Store and Google Play.

When you book, it helps to say:

  • Any stains you want treated, so the team can point them out at intake.
  • Whether you need anything back by a certain day.

For retail order support after booking, you can also email retail.support@dimalaundry.com.

What it costs

Dima Laundry charges per item, from a published price list that is the same at every branch. That means you can work out roughly what a bag will cost before the driver arrives. If you have a question about your pickup order, ask the team when you book.

Some standard prices for cleaning and pressing:

  • Shirt or T-shirt: AED 14
  • Trousers: AED 15
  • Two piece suit: AED 48
  • Abaya or kandura: AED 20
  • Bed sheet: AED 20
  • Duvet cover: AED 40
  • Bath towel: AED 10

If your clothes are already washed and only need pressing, pressing starts at AED 9.80 for a shirt. Premium service, with extra care and finishing for each piece, is priced separately. A two piece suit is AED 80 on premium.

Pickup is not only for clothes. You can add alterations to a laundry pickup, with minor alterations from AED 40 and fitting alterations from AED 60. Carpets and rugs can also be collected and delivered, from AED 30 per square metre. See the full price list for every item.

If you are not sure how an item will be priced, such as a wedding gown or a piece with heavy stains, ask the team before collection. Wedding gowns are inspected first and the price is confirmed before any cleaning starts.

Weekly pickups and express

There are two ways people in Dubai usually use pickup and delivery.

A fixed weekly pickup. You choose a fixed day and time and Dima Laundry collects every week. This suits families with a steady flow of school clothes, work shirts, bed linen and towels, and anyone who would rather not think about laundry at all. You leave the bag out, and it comes back.

Express when it cannot wait. For a suit before a meeting, an interview or an event, express service is available for pickup or for drop off at any branch. Express covers:

  • Shirts, trousers and suits
  • Dresses and blouses
  • Kanduras and abayas
  • Everyday laundry

Message the team on WhatsApp for today's express cut-off time and price, and book as early in the day as you can. Some items cannot be rushed safely: wedding dresses, leather, carpets, curtains, shoes and bags need standard turnaround. Carpets and rugs also need time to dry fully, and the team confirms the return date when collecting. More detail is on the express laundry page.

Tips for a smooth pickup

  • Check pockets. Pens, coins and tissues can damage clothes in the wash.
  • Separate the delicate pieces. Put silk, embellished abayas and anything labelled dry clean only in their own bag and mention them when you book.
  • Point out stains. Tell the team what the stain is if you know. Stains are treated before the main clean, and fresh ones come out more easily than old ones.
  • Leave clear instructions for the building. If the driver collects from reception or the concierge, let them know a laundry pickup is coming.
  • Plan around busy weeks. Demand for abayas and kanduras peaks before Ramadan and Eid, so book a few days early in those weeks.
  • Confirm the ready time. For anything you need on a certain day, ask the team to confirm when it will be back before you hand it over.
