Eid Laundry Rush: How to Book Pickup in Time

By the Dima Laundry team, 14 October 2026

Dubai laundries are busiest in the week before Eid, when kanduras, abayas, dresses and home linen all arrive at once. Book pickup five to seven days before Eid, send traditional wear first, and confirm the ready date when you book. If you leave it late, ask about express service.

Why the rush happens

Families prepare new and best clothes, guests are expected, and homes get a deep clean with curtains, rugs and sofa covers. All of that lands on laundries in the same few days.

What to send first

  • Kanduras, abayas, jalabiyas and dresses you will wear on the day.
  • Children's outfits.
  • Curtains and rugs, which take longest to dry.
  • Bed linen and towels for guests last, since they turn around fastest.

Booking tips

  • Book the pickup a week ahead.
  • List delicate or embellished items when you book so they are priced and handled correctly.
  • Confirm the ready date before you hand items over.
  • Use the Dima app or WhatsApp to track the order.

Frequently asked questions

When should I book laundry before Eid?

Five to seven days before Eid is safest. Curtains and rugs need longer.

Is express service available before Eid?

Dima Laundry offers express service. Ask on WhatsApp for the cut-off time, which can change in busy weeks.
