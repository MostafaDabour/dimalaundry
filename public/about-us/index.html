Serving Dubai Since 2001

Dima Laundry LLC is a Dubai laundry and dry cleaning company founded in 2001. Today it runs 8 retail branches and 3 processing centres in Al Quoz, cleans every item in-house, and serves households, hotels, holiday homes and businesses across the city.

Our story

Dima Laundry opened in Dubai in 2001 with a simple goal: clean clothes properly and return them on time. Over 25 years it grew into a network of branches inside the supermarkets and towers where Dubai residents already shop and live, from Dubai Marina to Karama.

Dima has always run its own cleaning operation. Every shirt, abaya, suit and bed sheet is cleaned by the Dima team in Al Quoz, never sent to a third party.

Then and now

  1. 2001 Dima Laundry begins serving Dubai.
  2. Today 8 retail branches open 10am to 10pm daily, 3 processing centres running 24 hours, pickup and delivery, the Dima app on iPhone and Android, and laundry for homes and businesses.
  3. Next A new processing centre in Dubai Investments Park 2, opening soon.
