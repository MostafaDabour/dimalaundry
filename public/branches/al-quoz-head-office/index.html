Dima Laundry Processing Centres, Al Quoz

Dima Laundry cleans every item in its own processing centres in Al Quoz, which run 24 hours. The head office is at 22, 24a Street, Al Quoz Industrial Area 3, with further centres in Al Quoz Industrial Area 4 and Al Quoz 3, and a new centre in Dubai Investments Park 2 opening soon.
