Commercial Laundry Services for Dubai Businesses

Dima Laundry provides commercial laundry in Dubai for hotels, holiday homes, restaurants, salons, spas, gyms, clinics and corporate uniforms. Businesses get scheduled pickups, per kg or per item pricing, itemised tracking and monthly invoicing, all processed in Dima's own Al Quoz facilities, which have handled B2B laundry since 2001.

Who we work with

  • Hotels and serviced apartments: bed linen, towels, guest laundry, staff uniforms.
  • Holiday homes and Airbnb hosts: turnover linen and towels between guests.
  • Restaurants and cafes: chef whites, aprons, napkins, tablecloths.
  • Salons, spas and gyms: towels, robes, gowns.
  • Corporate: staff uniforms for retail, security, hospitality, offices.
  • Clinics: scrubs, gowns, linen.

How business accounts work

  1. Free consultation and quote based on volume.
  2. Fixed pickup schedule: daily, alternate days or weekly.
  3. Items counted and tagged per collection, with a delivery note.
  4. Cleaned in Al Quoz and returned on the agreed turnaround.
  5. Monthly invoice with itemised usage.
